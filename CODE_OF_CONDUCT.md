# Code of conduct

DEVos welcomes respectful technical discussion from people with different levels of experience and backgrounds.

Project participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and specific when reviewing technical work.
- Critique ideas and code without personal attacks or harassment.
- Accept feedback and correct mistakes when evidence changes.
- Protect private device data and avoid sharing proprietary firmware or calibration files.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, and repeated disruptive conduct.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through GitHub. The maintainer may remove content or restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe and useful.
